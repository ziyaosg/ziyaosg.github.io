I am a second-year Ph.D. student in the Inquisitive Robotics Lab at Yale University, advised by Prof. Tesca Fitzgerald. My research interest lies in interactive robot learning, particularly in enabling robots to reason over human intentions and to learn from human interactions adaptively. My current projects focus on utilizing Vision-Language-Action Models to enhance robotics systems.

Prior to my Ph.D. program, I was a master's student in Computer Science at Yale University, advised by Prof. Tesca Fitzgerald and Prof. Arman Cohan. I received my bachelor's degree in Mathematics & Computer Science from New York University. During my undergraduate years, I was affiliated with NYU Robotic Design Team, advised by Prof. Haldun Hadimioglu.
